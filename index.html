How It Works & Honest Science

Deterministic solving. Every experiment record is generated by a fixed solver engine from its type and seed number — the same inputs always produce the same experiment, on any device, forever. That is why an experiment only ever needs solving once.

Honest simulations. Numbers in the measurement tables are computed from built-in physical and statistical models (projectile motion, Hooke’s law, Newton’s cooling, titration curves and the like) to illustrate the method. They are clearly labeled SOLVER SIMULATION and are never presented as laboratory-measured data. Survey-style experiments use seeded illustrative distributions.

Safety. Experiments involving heat, chemicals, electricity, or projectiles carry explicit safety notes. Young scientists work with adult supervision.
